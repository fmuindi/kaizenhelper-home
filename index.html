The problem

Improvement work breaks down when issues live in spreadsheets, notes, and scattered conversations.

Most teams know exactly where problems happen on the floor. What breaks down is everything after that — capturing issues consistently, understanding the real cause, and following through until the issue is actually solved. Good ideas get logged once, then quietly disappear.

Issues scatter across spreadsheets, sticky notes, emails, and meeting minutes.
Root causes stay unclear — teams treat symptoms instead of the source.
Follow-ups get lost with no owner, due date, or visible status.
The same problems return because nothing closed the loop the first time.

The difference isn't more effort — it's giving every problem one place to live, one cause to fix, and one owner to close it out.

The workflow

Capture. Analyze. Act. Sustain.

A simple loop that turns a problem reported on the floor into a countermeasure that sticks.

01

Capture

Teams log abnormal conditions, waste, gaps, observations, and follow-up items as they happen.

02

Analyze

AI helps identify patterns, likely root causes, and improvement opportunities.

03

Act

Assign countermeasures, owners, due dates, and follow-up actions.

04

Sustain

Track completion, recurring issues, trends, and leadership visibility over time.
